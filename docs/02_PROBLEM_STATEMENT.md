# 02. Problem Statement

## Context
Ecommerce merchants operate in a low-margin, high-velocity environment. Their profitability is constantly threatened by moving variables: ad performance drops, out-of-stock items, changing fulfillment costs, and shifting consumer behavior.

## The Core Pain Point
Merchants are **data rich but insight poor**. 

They have data scattered across:
- Shopify (Orders, Customers, Inventory)
- Meta Ads (Spend, CPM, CPA, ROAS)

Finding profit leaks requires manually joining this data and looking for anomalies. Most merchants do not have the time or the data engineering skills to do this effectively. By the time they realize a Meta ad is burning money on an out-of-stock product, the profit is already gone.

## The Solution
A system that automatically ingest this data, actively monitors it against proven profitability rules, and generates human-readable alerts and action plans. We are the automated fractional CTO/CMO for the merchant.
